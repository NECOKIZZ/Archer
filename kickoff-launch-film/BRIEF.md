# Kickoff launch film: brief

**Business:** kickoff.cash runs proximity markets on English Premier League fixtures. You predict a number (the final scoreline), stake USDC into a shared pool, and the pool pays the traders who landed closest. There is no house: your counterparty is the rest of the pool.

**Buyer / viewer:** football fans who already have opinions on scorelines: fantasy (FPL) players, prediction-market and crypto traders, and people who find yes/no betting too blunt. Most are on their phones, scrolling X, with the sound off.

**Viewer's problem:** binary markets throw away graded knowledge. Calling 2-1 instead of 4-0 is real insight, but "Arsenal win" pays both the same.

**The single action (CTA):** Join the waitlist at **kickoff.cash**. Access opens in waves, by invite code.

## What the audience needs to know, in order
1. **The hook:** football isn't yes or no, so your prediction shouldn't be either.
2. **What it is:** proximity markets for every Premier League match.
3. **How you play:** pick a scoreline and stake USDC before kickoff.
4. **The differentiator:** close still pays. Payouts scale with how close you land.
5. **How it settles:** the closer half of the field wins (the median gate), and the pool splits by stake × accuracy, so an exact call takes the biggest share.
6. **Why it's fair:** no house; the gate counts heads, not dollars; the fee comes only from losing stakes.
7. **Why it's trustworthy:** a market settles when two independent data sources agree, usually within minutes of full time.
8. **Why you keep coming back:** 5% of fees feed the Season Accumulator, shared by the season's top 20 players; you can also run an AI agent that trades on the same terms.
9. **What to do now:** join the waitlist at kickoff.cash.

## Provably true (source: kickoff.cash and kickoff.cash/docs, read 2026-10-01)
- Proximity markets on EPL fixtures, with one market per match, listed automatically about a week before kickoff.
- Pick a scoreline and stake USDC; you can edit the pick until kickoff, when the market locks.
- Median gate: positions strictly closer than the median distance win, counted per trader, not per dollar.
- Split: stake × accuracy weight; exact calls get the maximum weight. Winners keep their stake.
- Worked example from the docs: five $10 stakes, the match ends 2-1. A (2-1) gets ≈ +232%, B (1-0) gets ≈ +38%, and C, D and E lose.
- Fee: 10% of losing stakes only, split 5% platform and 5% Season Accumulator. No deposit, withdrawal or per-trade fees.
- Season Accumulator: shared among the season's top 20 players (homepage).
- Settlement: two independent sources must agree, usually within minutes of full time. Disputes hold the market; a void market refunds everyone in full.
- One AI agent per account, trading on the same terms (soul.md, or bring your own AI).
- The "first proximity market on Robinhood Chain" (homepage claim, quoted as the brand's own).
- **Current status:** testnet season with play money and test USDC; access by waitlist and invite waves.

## Never claim
- Real-money winnings today, guaranteed profit, odds, or projected payouts (Kickoff itself never shows payout estimates).
- That Player Perps are live: **they are paused**, so they're left out of the film.
- Real users, testimonials, volumes or pool sizes. All UI is illustrative and labelled as such.
- Club crests, player likenesses or Premier League marks. Clubs appear only as three-letter codes in illustrative UI.
