# Kickoff launch film: storyboard (60 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps)

**Look:** borrowed from the Kalshi Perpetuals reference, but only its mechanisms: a warm near-black canvas, one brand-colour glow rising from below, short sentence-case captions in thin type, dark UI cards shot close up, and a 3D brand object that bookends the film.

**Colours:** everything is in Kickoff's own palette. Green `#00C805` means you and winning. Purple `#7B62F6` means the field, the data and the crowd. Cream `#F7F5F0` is the type.

**Persistent actor:** the green **ball** from the Kickoff "K" mark. It is the dot that ignites the logo, the cursor that taps a score, the slider thumb, the live position marker, your dot in the field, and the coin that feeds the Accumulator.

**Three signature transformations**
1. Two different scorelines crush together into one grey "ARS WIN" pill. Its status dot ignites green and becomes the ball of the K logo.
2. The logo's ball grows into a portal that reveals the score grid. The tapped 2-1 cell then expands into the market card, carrying "2–1" to the exact same pixels.
3. The live-PnL line's endpoint drops into the field of traders as one dot left of the median. The winners' dots stretch into the payout bars, and the fee slivers stream into the Season Accumulator.

**Pacing:** 13 scenes and about 22 distinct compositions. Beats run 1.4–3.6 s, and every hold has a slow 2–4% push.
**Audio:** a dark minimal electronic bed at 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s), with a riser into the logo at 9.6 s, a drop at 13 s, a breakdown at 42 s and a final swell. Effects are one soft whoosh per real transition, ticks only on real UI actions (tap, slide, lock), and a settle chime at 44.5 s. Targets are −14 LUFS and a true peak of −1 dBTP or lower.
